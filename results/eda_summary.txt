CUSTOMER CHURN PROJECT - EDA SUMMARY
====================================

Dataset:
- Total customers: 500
- Total features: 17
- Churned customers: 317 (63.4%)
- Non-churned customers: 183 (36.6%)

KEY FINDINGS
------------

1. Contract Type
- Monthly contract churn rate: 84.36%
- Yearly contract churn rate: 49.04%
- 2-Year contract churn rate: 11.76%
- Monthly contract customers show the highest churn rate.

2. Subscription Type
- Basic churn rate: 64.71%
- Premium churn rate: 64.52%
- Standard churn rate: 60.87%
- Churn rates are relatively similar across subscription types.

3. Internet Service
- DSL churn rate: 62.21%
- Fiber churn rate: 69.39%
- No Internet churn rate: 59.67%

4. Gender
- Female churn rate: 62.4%
- Male churn rate: 64.4%
- Churn rates are similar between genders.

5. Customer Behavior
- Average inactive days:
  No churn: 24.68
  Churn: 33.89

- Average support calls:
  No churn: 3.34
  Churn: 4.42

- Average satisfaction score:
  No churn: 6.02
  Churn: 5.24

- Average monthly charges:
  No churn: 69.92
  Churn: 76.70

- Average tenure:
  No churn: 34.75 months
  Churn: 28.72 months

CONCLUSION
----------
The EDA indicates that contract type, inactive days,
satisfaction score, support calls, monthly charges,
and tenure may be useful features for predicting customer churn.

These observations will be evaluated further using
machine learning models.
